# 四福音合參（和合本）
### Synopsis of the Four Gospels in Chinese (Chinese Union Version, 1919)

A free, browser-based synopsis of the four Gospels in Traditional Chinese. The pericopes are arranged and numbered after Kurt Aland’s *Synopsis Quattuor Evangeliorum*, and the text is the Chinese Union Version (和合本) of 1919.

The whole synopsis is a single self-contained file, `四福音合參_和合本.html`. Open it in any browser on a computer, tablet or phone. No account, installation or internet connection is needed (without internet, the browser’s own Chinese serif font replaces Noto Serif TC).

## Features

### Arrangement
- **367 sections** in the order and numbering of Aland’s synopsis (e.g. § 93 = 太 9:9–13 // 可 2:13–17 // 路 5:27–32), grouped under the 18 major divisions, with every section and division title in Traditional Chinese using 和合本 terminology (文士, 長大痲瘋的, 污鬼, 公會, etc.).
- **Contents page (目錄)** listing every section with its 太, 可, 路 and 約 references. A **bold** reference marks the Gospel whose own sequence the section follows.
- **Cross-references:** a parallel repeated from elsewhere carries a link (→ §) to the section where it stands in its own sequence.
- **Direct links** to any section by adding its number to the address, e.g. `#s93`, or `#contents` for the contents page.

### Synoptic layout
- **Parallel columns** in canonical order (馬太福音, 馬可福音, 路加福音, 約翰福音), showing only the Gospels present in each section. § 361 adds 林前 15:3–8.
- **Aligned layout (對齊):** the text is divided into phrases at Chinese punctuation (，。；：？！、), and parallel phrases are set on the same line across the columns, with blank space where a Gospel lacks wording found in the others, after the manner of Aland’s synopsis. Hovering over a line highlights the whole row.
- **Running text (連續經文):** an alternative layout showing each column as continuous text.
- **和合本 notes:** the small-print notes of the 和合本 (e.g. 〔有古卷作：文士和法利賽人〕) are shown in smaller grey type and are ignored when lines are matched.

### Tools
- **Search** by title (e.g. “利未”) or by passage, in Chinese or English (e.g. “路 5:27” or “Lk 5:27” lists every section containing that verse).
- **Text size (小字 / 中字 / 大字)** and automatic light and dark themes.
- **Print / Save as PDF (列印)** any range of sections, one landscape page per section.

## How to use

1. Open the contents page and select a section, or type a section number in the box at the top.
2. Use ‹ and › (or the left and right arrow keys) to move between sections.
3. Use the toolbar to switch between 對齊 and 連續經文, change the text size, or print.

## Notes on the text

- The 和合本 includes verses that modern critical editions omit (e.g. 太 18:11, 可 9:44, 約 5:4); these appear in full.
- In the source text, 約 7:53 is joined to 8:1; it has been separated and restored as 7:53.
- Mark’s shorter ending (§ 362) is not part of the 和合本 and is shown as “—”.

## Limitations

- **Alignment is automatic, not editorial.** Phrases are matched by shared two-character word pairs. It works well where the wording is close; where parallels are loose or the order differs widely, some lines may pair imperfectly or sit a row apart. Alignment is by phrase, not word by word.
- Half-verse references (e.g. 1:14a) display the whole verse.
- No critical apparatus, Gospel of Thomas, or patristic parallels.
